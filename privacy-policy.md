# KRAVY — Privacy Policy

## 1. Data Collection

KRAVY may collect and store information necessary for its features, including:

- Discord user and server IDs.
- Moderation records and security logs.
- Support ticket messages and transcripts.
- Server settings, roles, and permissions.
- Information submitted through forms and applications.

## 2. Data Usage

Collected data is used exclusively for server management, moderation, security, and support features.

## 3. Data Storage

Data may be stored locally in databases and configuration files. Access is limited to the bot operator and authorized server staff.

## 4. Data Sharing

KRAVY does not sell personal data. Information may be accessible to authorized staff and processed by Discord as part of its services.

## 5. Data Retention

Data is retained as necessary for the bot's functionality and server administration. Deletion requests may be submitted to the bot operator.

## 6. User Rights

Users may request access to or deletion of their personal data by contacting the server administration.

## 7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information from unauthorized access.

## 8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the KRAVY administrator through the Discord server where the bot operates.
