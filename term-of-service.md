KRAVY is a Discord bot designed for server management, moderation, security, and community support.

Users must comply with Discord's Terms of Service and the rules of the server where KRAVY operates.

Server administrators are responsible for configuring KRAVY and managing moderation actions performed through the bot.

## 4. Data and Privacy

KRAVY processes information necessary to provide its features. For more information, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

## 5. Availability

KRAVY may be updated, modified, or temporarily unavailable without prior notice.

## 6. Contact

For questions or support, please contact the KRAVY administrator through the Discord server where the bot operates.
